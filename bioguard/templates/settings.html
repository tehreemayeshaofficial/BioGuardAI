BioGuard AI

Hospital infection surveillance, antimicrobial resistance monitoring, and outbreak-risk assessment.

How surveillance works

Uploaded laboratory reports are processed into isolate-level surveillance data. Trends, resistance patterns, ward distribution, and outbreak indicators are calculated from the available dataset.

Risk interpretation

  • Low — routine surveillance
  • Moderate — increased monitoring
  • High — infection-control review recommended
  • Critical — urgent review/action according to hospital policy

Antimicrobial resistance

  • MDR — non-susceptibility in at least 3 antimicrobial categories.
  • XDR — non-susceptibility in all but 2 or fewer antimicrobial categories.
  • PDR — non-susceptibility to every tested agent.

Data & AI note

AI-assisted analysis supports surveillance and should be interpreted by qualified infection-control/laboratory personnel. BioGuard AI does not replace clinical judgment or hospital policy.
